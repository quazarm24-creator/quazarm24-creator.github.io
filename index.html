Rules

Keep it stellar

A few simple rules so everyone has a good time.

Be respectful

No harassment, hate speech or drama. Everyone started as a beginner.

No piracy

We mod games we own. No cracked games or paid mods shared without permission.

Credit creators

Always credit the original author when sharing or editing someone else's mod.

Safe downloads only

No malware, sketchy links or re-uploads. Link to the official source.

Use the right channel

Help questions go in help channels, mods go in mod channels.

Keep it SFW

No NSFW content anywhere, including mod screenshots and avatars.

FAQ

Questions?

Do I need to know how to mod to join?

Nope. Lots of members just want to find good mods and play. We'll help you install them.

Is the server free?

Yes, joining Lunar! is completely free.

Can I post my own mods?

Absolutely. Share them in the game's mod channel with a description, screenshots and an official download link.

My game isn't listed. Can I still join?

Of course. Post in the suggestions channel. If there's interest, we'll add a section for it.

Do you help with Quest / standalone VR modding?

Yes. BONELAB and other VR games have both PC and Quest modding covered in their help channels.
